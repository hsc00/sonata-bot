These are the Sonata bible commands:
# Setup
- `setfm <username>`: Set your last.fm username. ✅
- `setrym <username>`: Set your RYM username. ✅
- `setspotify`: Set your connection between Spotify and Sonata bot.
- `i or import`: Import your RYM or AOTY ratings to the bot. Check instructions [here]. ✅

# Albums
- `ab or album`: Check RYM info for an album or release. ✅
- `wa or whoknowsalbum`: Check who rated a release ✅
- `ratingchanges or rc`: Check the rating changes for a release. ⌛
- `rdr (roast/glaze) or randomrating` (roast/glaze): Check a random rating/roast/glaze from available users. ✅
- `newreleases` : Searches for new releases. ✅
- `mrr or mostratedreleases` : Checks the server most rated albums. ✅
- `brr or bestratedreleases` : Checks the server best rated albums. ✅
- `brr or bestratedreleases` user : Checks the user best rated albums. ✅
- `wrr or worstratedreleases` user : Checks the user best rated albums.✅
- `wrr or worstratedreleases`: Check the worst rated releases. ✅
- 'history`: check a release rating history (use a TTL cache, after a cache hit when the cache is invalid update the rating, probably a TTL of 7 days is the best)

# Artists
- `st or setlist`: Check an artist last concert setlist.
- `sts or setlists`: Get the info and links to an artist latest concerts setlists.
- `inf` or `influences`: Check an artist influences.
- `fl` or `followers`: Check artists influenced by an artist.
- `ar or artistratings (user)` : Checks your artist ratings. ✅
- `mra or mostratedartists` : Checks the server most rated artists. ✅
- `bra or bestratedartists (user)` : Checks the best rated artists. ✅
- `wra or worstratedartists (user)` : Checks the worst rated artists. ✅

# Tracks
- `tr` or `trackinfo`: Check track info.
- `samples`: Check samples and interpolations used/being used in a track. ✅
- `covers`: Check if a track is a cover or has covers.
- `lyrics` or `lr`: Check the current lyrics for the current or a specific track. ✅

# User Stats 
- `profile`: Check your own or another person's profile. ✅
- `users`: Check RYM users list. ✅
- `rr` or `ratingsrank`: Checks the ratings rank. ✅
- `rg` or `ratingsglazers`: Checks the ratings glazers rank. ✅
- `rh` or `ratingshaters`: Checks the ratings haters rank. ✅
- `aoty (year) (user)`: Checks the highest rated releases from a year. ✅
- `laoty (year) (user)`: Checks the lowest rated releases from a year. ✅
