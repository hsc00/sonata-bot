These are the Sonata bible commands:
# Setup
- `!setfm <username>`: Set your last.fm username.
- `!setrym <username>`: Set your RYM username.
- `!i or !import`: Import your RYM ratings to the bot. Check instructions [here].
- `!pingsonata`: Check if the bot is online.




# Albums
- `!ab or !album`: Check RYM info for an album or release.
- `!wa or !whoknowsalbum`: Check who rated a release
- `!ratingchanges or !rc`: Check the rating changes for a release.
- `!rdr or !randomrating`: Check a random rating from available users.
- `!wlr or !wholikesrelease`: Check who likes a release.
- `!mlr or !mostlovedreleases`: Check the most loved releases.
- `!wdr or !whodislikesrelease`: Check who dislikes a release.
- `!mhr or !mosthatedreleases`: Check the most hated releases.

# Artists
- `!a or !artist`: Check RYM and last.fm info for an artist.
- `!st or !setlist`: Check an artist last concert setlist.
- `!sts or !setlists`: Get the info and links to an artist latest concerts setlists.
- `!inf` or `!influences`: Check an artist influences.
- `!fl` or `!followers`: Check artists influenced by an artist.
- `!wla or !wholikesartist`: Check who likes an artist.
- `!mla or !mostlovedartists`: Check the most loved artists.
- `!wda or !whodislikesartist`: Check who dislikes an artist.
- `!mha or !mosthatedartists`: Check the most hated artists.
# Tracks
- `!tr` or `trackinfo`: Check a track info.
- `!samples`: Check a track samples used/being used in.








# Profile 
- `!profile`: Check your own or another person's profile.
- `!users`: Check RYM users list.
- `!ratings`: Check your rating curve.
